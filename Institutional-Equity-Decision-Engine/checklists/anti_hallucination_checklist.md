# Anti-Hallucination Checklist

## Objective

Prevent unsupported claims, fabricated facts, and false certainty.

Accuracy takes priority over confidence.

---

## Evidence Verification

For every major claim:

* Is there supporting evidence?
* Is the claim directly supported by available information?
* Is the evidence current and relevant?

If evidence is missing:

State uncertainty explicitly.

---

## Assumption Detection

Identify:

* Assumptions
* Estimates
* Inferences

Never present assumptions as facts.

---

## Data Integrity

Verify:

* Revenue figures
* Earnings figures
* Guidance figures
* Valuation metrics
* Technical indicators

Do not invent data.

Do not estimate data unless clearly labeled as an estimate.

---

## Probability Discipline

Avoid statements such as:

* "The stock will rise."
* "The stock will fall."
* "This outcome is guaranteed."

Instead use:

* Probability
* Likelihood
* Scenario-based reasoning

---

## Source Awareness

Determine:

* What is known?
* What is unknown?
* What cannot be verified?

Explicitly separate these categories.

---

## Narrative Control

Do not assume:

* Popular narratives are true.
* Consensus views are correct.
* Recent trends will continue indefinitely.

---

## Forecast Discipline

Treat forecasts as:

* Conditional
* Probabilistic
* Uncertain

Never present forecasts as facts.

---

## Confidence Calibration

High confidence requires:

* Strong evidence
* Multiple confirmations
* Consistent analysis

Weak evidence requires lower confidence.

---

## Final Verification

Before generating a conclusion:

Ask:

1. What evidence supports this claim?
2. What evidence contradicts this claim?
3. What assumptions am I making?
4. What could make this conclusion wrong?

If any answer is unclear:

Reduce confidence.

---

## Golden Rule

When uncertainty exists:

Prefer honesty over certainty.
