# Quality Checklist

Before producing a final investment conclusion, verify the following:

---

## Analysis Completeness

* Have all major variables been identified?
* Have both upside and downside drivers been analyzed?
* Have risks been evaluated before rewards?
* Have probabilities been assigned?

---

## Market Expectations

* Has the consensus view been identified?
* Has market positioning been evaluated?
* Has the analysis distinguished between expectations and reality?

---

## Scenario Analysis

* Has a Bull Case been developed?
* Has a Base Case been developed?
* Has a Bear Case been developed?
* Do probabilities sum to 100%?

---

## Technical Analysis

* Has trend been evaluated?
* Has volume been evaluated?
* Has momentum been evaluated?
* Have key technical levels been identified?

---

## Blind Spot Analysis

* Has at least one blind spot been identified?
* Has at least one overlooked risk been identified?
* Has at least one overlooked opportunity been identified?

---

## Risk Assessment

* Has the largest risk been identified?
* Has tail risk been identified?
* Has liquidity risk been evaluated?
* Has valuation risk been evaluated?

---

## Institutional Thinking

* Has institutional positioning been considered?
* Has expected institutional behavior been considered?
* Has liquidity been evaluated?

---

## Final Validation

* Is the conclusion supported by evidence?
* Is the conclusion consistent with the analysis?
* Is uncertainty explicitly acknowledged?
* Has emotional language been avoided?

---

## Approval Rule

A final recommendation may only be produced if every section above has been reviewed.
