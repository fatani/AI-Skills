# Research Framework

## Objective

Ensure that conclusions are built on the most current and verifiable information available.

Thinking quality cannot compensate for outdated data.

Research precedes analysis.

Verification precedes conviction.

---

## Research Priority Order

Always attempt to gather information in the following order:

1. Company Filings
2. Earnings Reports
3. Earnings Call Transcripts
4. Official Company Guidance
5. Regulatory Filings
6. Industry Data
7. Institutional Ownership Data
8. Market Data
9. Analyst Consensus
10. Media Reports

Higher-priority sources override lower-priority sources.

---

## Data Freshness Assessment

Classify all data:

### Current

Definition:

Within the relevant decision window.

Examples:

* Current stock price
* Latest earnings
* Latest guidance
* Latest institutional ownership

Label:

[Current]

---

### Recent

Definition:

Likely still useful but not current.

Examples:

* Prior quarter earnings
* Older guidance
* Historical ownership data

Label:

[Recent]

---

### Historical

Definition:

Useful for context only.

Examples:

* Prior cycles
* Historical margins
* Historical valuation ranges

Label:

[Historical]

---

### Stale

Definition:

Too old for decision making.

Examples:

* Expired forecasts
* Obsolete industry assumptions

Label:

[Stale]

---

## Verification Protocol

For every major variable determine:

* Verified
* Partially Verified
* Unverified

Examples:

Price:
[Verified]

RSI:
[Verified]

Institutional Ownership:
[Partially Verified]

Future Demand:
[Unverified]

---

## Source Quality Ranking

Score sources:

5 = Primary Source

Examples:

* SEC filings
* Earnings reports
* Company guidance

4 = High Quality Secondary Source

Examples:

* Industry reports
* Exchange data

3 = Analyst Interpretation

Examples:

* Sell-side research
* Consensus estimates

2 = Media Interpretation

Examples:

* Financial journalism

1 = Speculation

Examples:

* Social media
* Rumors
* Anonymous commentary

---

## Missing Data Detection

Always identify:

* Missing Variables
* Missing Sources
* Missing Verification

Questions:

* What information is unavailable?
* What information would materially change the conclusion?

---

## Research Gap Analysis

Categorize gaps:

### Critical Gap

Can change the investment thesis.

Examples:

* Current earnings
* Current guidance
* Current regulatory status

---

### Important Gap

Can change conviction level.

Examples:

* Current ownership
* Current valuation

---

### Minor Gap

Helpful but not thesis-changing.

Examples:

* Short-term sentiment
* Minor estimate revisions

---

## Institutional Research Standards

Before issuing a recommendation verify:

* Current Price
* Market Capitalization
* Latest Earnings
* Latest Guidance
* Current Valuation
* Current Industry Conditions
* Current Competitive Landscape

If unavailable:

Explicitly state:

[Research Incomplete]

---

## Confidence Restrictions

Confidence may not exceed:

### High

Unless:

* Data is Current
* Data is Verified
* Source Quality >= 4

### Medium

If:

* Data is Recent
* Partially Verified

### Low

If:

* Data is Historical
* Unverified
* Source Quality <= 3

---

## Final Assessment

For every major conclusion provide:

* Data Freshness
* Verification Status
* Source Quality
* Research Completeness

Always ask:

"Would this conclusion still survive if current data were available?"
