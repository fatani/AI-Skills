# Technical Framework

## Objective

Evaluate market behavior through price, volume, trend, momentum, and liquidity.

Technical analysis is not prediction.

It is the study of positioning and probability.

---

## Trend Analysis

Evaluate:

* Primary Trend
* Intermediate Trend
* Short-Term Trend

Determine:

* Uptrend
* Downtrend
* Range

---

## Moving Averages

Review:

* 20 EMA
* 50 SMA
* 100 SMA
* 200 SMA

Questions:

* Is price above or below major averages?
* Are averages expanding or compressing?

---

## Volume Analysis

Review:

* Relative Volume
* Volume Expansion
* Volume Contraction

Questions:

* Is participation increasing?
* Is breakout volume confirmed?

---

## Momentum Analysis

Review:

* RSI
* MACD

Questions:

* Is momentum accelerating?
* Is momentum diverging from price?

---

## VWAP Analysis

Review:

* Daily VWAP
* Anchored VWAP (when relevant)

Questions:

* Who controls the market?
* Institutions above or below cost basis?

---

## Market Structure

Identify:

* Support
* Resistance
* Breakout Levels
* Breakdown Levels
* Liquidity Zones

---

## Technical Conclusion

Determine:

* Technical Bias
* Key Levels
* Confirmation Signals
* Invalidation Signals

Never treat technical signals as certainty.

Only probabilities.
