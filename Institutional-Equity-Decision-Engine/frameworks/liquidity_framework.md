# Liquidity Framework

## Objective

Track the movement of capital across markets, sectors, industries, and themes.

Price is ultimately driven by liquidity.

Fundamentals determine long-term value.

Liquidity determines timing.

---

## Liquidity Mapping

Determine where capital is flowing:

### Market Level

Examples:

* Equities
* Bonds
* Commodities
* Cash

Questions:

* Is liquidity entering risk assets?
* Is liquidity leaving risk assets?

---

### Sector Level

Examples:

* Technology
* Financials
* Healthcare
* Energy

Questions:

* Which sectors are attracting capital?
* Which sectors are losing capital?

---

### Industry Level

Examples:

* Semiconductors
* Software
* Networking
* Data Center Infrastructure

Questions:

* Which industries are being re-rated?
* Which industries are being de-rated?

---

### Theme Level

Examples:

* AI Compute
* AI Memory
* AI Optical
* AI Power
* AI Networking

Questions:

* Which AI layer is attracting the next wave of capital?
* Which layer has already been crowded?

---

## Relative Liquidity Analysis

Determine:

* Liquidity Winners
* Liquidity Losers

Questions:

* Which companies are receiving disproportionate capital inflows?
* Which companies are being ignored?

---

## Rotation Analysis

Identify:

* Source of Liquidity
* Destination of Liquidity

Questions:

* Where is money coming from?
* Where is money going?

Examples:

* GPU → Memory
* Memory → Optical
* Optical → Power
* Power → Networking

---

## Institutional Flow Analysis

Evaluate:

* ETF Flows
* Mutual Fund Flows
* Hedge Fund Positioning
* Sovereign Capital Flows

Questions:

* Which institutions are buying?
* Which institutions are selling?

---

## Re-Rating Analysis

Determine:

* Current Re-Rating
* Potential Next Re-Rating

Questions:

* What is already priced?
* What is not yet priced?

---

## Liquidity Risk

Evaluate:

* Crowding Risk
* Forced Selling Risk
* Liquidity Shock Risk

Questions:

* What happens if liquidity reverses?
* Who becomes the marginal seller?

---

## AI Capital Rotation

For AI-related companies determine:

1. Current Bottleneck
2. Current Capital Destination
3. Next Capital Destination
4. Most Ignored Layer
5. Most Crowded Layer

---

## Final Assessment

Determine:

* Current Liquidity Direction
* Liquidity Strength
* Re-Rating Potential
* Crowding Risk
* Next Capital Rotation

Always ask:

"Where is the next dollar likely to go?"
