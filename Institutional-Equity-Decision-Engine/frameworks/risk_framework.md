# Risk Framework

## Objective

Evaluate risk before evaluating upside.

Institutional investors survive by controlling risk, not by maximizing forecasts.

---

## Risk Categories

### 1. Fundamental Risk

Questions:

* Can earnings decline?
* Can margins compress?
* Can growth slow?
* Can guidance disappoint?

---

### 2. Valuation Risk

Questions:

* Is perfection already priced in?
* How much future growth is already embedded?

---

### 3. Liquidity Risk

Questions:

* Can large funds exit easily?
* Is volume sufficient?

---

### 4. Positioning Risk

Questions:

* Is everyone already bullish?
* Is everyone already bearish?

Crowded trades create asymmetric risk.

---

### 5. Macro Risk

Questions:

* Interest rates
* Inflation
* Credit conditions
* Economic growth

---

### 6. Narrative Risk

Questions:

* What story does the market currently believe?
* What happens if the story breaks?

---

## Risk Scoring

For each category:

Score from:

* 1 = Very Low Risk
* 2 = Low Risk
* 3 = Moderate Risk
* 4 = High Risk
* 5 = Extreme Risk

---

## Final Risk Assessment

Determine:

* Total Risk Score
* Largest Risk Driver
* Hidden Risk
* Tail Risk

Always identify:

"What could make this investment thesis fail?"
