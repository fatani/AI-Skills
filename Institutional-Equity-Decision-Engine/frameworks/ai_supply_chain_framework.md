# AI Supply Chain Framework

## Objective

Analyze AI-related companies through their position within the AI infrastructure and supply chain ecosystem.

Avoid evaluating AI companies in isolation.

Understand where value is being created, where bottlenecks exist, and where capital is flowing.

---

## AI Value Chain Mapping

Determine where the company operates:

### Compute

Examples:

* GPU
* CPU
* ASIC
* TPU

Questions:

* Does the company provide compute?
* Is it exposed to training or inference demand?

---

### Memory & Storage

Examples:

* HBM
* DRAM
* NAND
* SSD
* HDD

Questions:

* Is memory a bottleneck?
* Is pricing power improving or weakening?

---

### Optical & Networking

Examples:

* Optical Modules
* DSP
* LPO
* CPO
* InfiniBand
* Ethernet

Questions:

* Is bandwidth becoming a constraint?
* Is optical demand accelerating?

---

### PCB & Electronic Infrastructure

Examples:

* PCB
* PCBA
* HDI
* CCL

Questions:

* Is complexity increasing?
* Is AI driving higher content per system?

---

### Power & Cooling

Examples:

* PSU
* BBU
* HVDC
* Cooling Infrastructure

Questions:

* Is power becoming a bottleneck?
* Is infrastructure spending accelerating?

---

### AI Platforms & CSPs

Examples:

* Cloud Providers
* AI Service Providers
* Foundation Models

Questions:

* Who captures the economic value?
* Who bears the capital expenditure?

---

## Bottleneck Analysis

Identify:

* Current Bottleneck
* Emerging Bottleneck
* Resolved Bottleneck

Questions:

* Where is supply constrained?
* Where is demand strongest?

---

## Capital Flow Analysis

Determine:

* Where capital is entering
* Where capital is leaving

Questions:

* Which layer of the AI stack is being re-rated?
* Which layer is being ignored?

---

## AI Cycle Assessment

Evaluate:

* Early Cycle
* Mid Cycle
* Late Cycle

Questions:

* Where are we in the current AI investment cycle?
* Which segment benefits next?

---

## Competitive Position

Determine:

* Leader
* Challenger
* Supplier
* Commodity Provider

Questions:

* Does the company possess pricing power?
* Does the company control a critical bottleneck?

---

## Risk Assessment

Evaluate:

* Technology Risk
* Capacity Expansion Risk
* Demand Risk
* Competition Risk

---

## Final Assessment

Determine:

* Position In AI Value Chain
* Key Bottleneck Exposure
* Pricing Power
* Competitive Advantage
* Most Important AI Variable

Always ask:

"Where will the next AI bottleneck emerge?"
