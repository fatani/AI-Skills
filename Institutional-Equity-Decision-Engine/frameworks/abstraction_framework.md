# Abstraction Framework

## Objective

Move beyond company-specific details and identify the higher-level forces driving outcomes.

Avoid getting trapped in surface-level analysis.

Focus on systems, incentives, and structural dynamics.

---

## Layer 1 — Company

Evaluate:

* Revenue
* Earnings
* Margins
* Guidance
* Execution

Question:

* What is happening inside the company?

---

## Layer 2 — Industry

Evaluate:

* Industry growth
* Industry profitability
* Competitive structure
* Supply and demand

Question:

* What is happening inside the industry?

---

## Layer 3 — Ecosystem

Evaluate:

* Suppliers
* Customers
* Partners
* Distribution channels

Question:

* What is happening around the company?

---

## Layer 4 — Macro Environment

Evaluate:

* Interest rates
* Inflation
* Economic growth
* Credit conditions
* Geopolitical factors

Question:

* What external forces matter most?

---

## Layer 5 — Structural Trends

Evaluate:

* Technological shifts
* Demographic shifts
* Regulatory shifts
* Capital allocation trends

Question:

* What long-term forces are shaping outcomes?

---

## Incentive Analysis

Identify incentives for:

* Management
* Customers
* Suppliers
* Competitors
* Investors

Question:

* Who benefits and why?

---

## System Mapping

Map:

* Inputs
* Outputs
* Bottlenecks
* Feedback loops

Question:

* What is the real system behind the stock?

---

## Final Abstraction

Determine:

* Core Driver
* Structural Tailwind
* Structural Headwind
* Most Important Long-Term Variable

Always ask:

"If this stock were merely a symptom, what larger system would explain its behavior?"
