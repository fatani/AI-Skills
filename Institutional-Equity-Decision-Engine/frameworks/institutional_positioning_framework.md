# Institutional Positioning Framework

## Objective

Determine how investors are positioned and how positioning may influence future price movements.

Price does not move solely because of fundamentals.

Price also moves because of positioning.

---

## Ownership Analysis

Evaluate:

* Institutional Ownership
* Insider Ownership
* Retail Ownership
* Passive Ownership

Questions:

* Who owns the stock?
* What type of investor dominates ownership?

---

## Crowding Analysis

Determine:

* Is the trade crowded?
* Is sentiment excessively bullish?
* Is sentiment excessively bearish?

Questions:

* How many investors already agree with the thesis?
* Who is left to buy?
* Who is left to sell?

---

## Positioning Risk

Evaluate:

* Long Positioning Risk
* Short Positioning Risk

Questions:

* Is the stock over-owned?
* Is the stock under-owned?

---

## Flow Analysis

Evaluate:

* Institutional Buying
* Institutional Selling
* ETF Flows
* Passive Flows

Questions:

* Where is money moving?
* What type of capital is entering or leaving?

---

## Short Interest Analysis

Review:

* Short Interest
* Days To Cover
* Borrow Conditions

Questions:

* Is there squeeze potential?
* Is there informed bearish positioning?

---

## Liquidity Analysis

Evaluate:

* Average Daily Volume
* Market Depth
* Exit Capacity

Questions:

* Can institutions enter easily?
* Can institutions exit easily?

---

## Positioning Scenarios

### Bullish Positioning

Conditions:

* Under-owned
* Improving fundamentals
* Increasing institutional demand

---

### Bearish Positioning

Conditions:

* Over-owned
* Weakening fundamentals
* Institutional distribution

---

## Final Assessment

Determine:

* Ownership Quality
* Positioning Risk
* Crowding Risk
* Flow Direction
* Positioning Advantage

Always ask:

"Who still needs to buy?"

and

"Who may be forced to sell?"
