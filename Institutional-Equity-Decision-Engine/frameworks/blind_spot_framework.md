# Blind Spot Framework

## Objective

Identify risks, opportunities, and assumptions that the market may be overlooking.

The greatest investment opportunities often emerge from what the market ignores.

---

## Consensus Analysis

Determine:

* What does the majority believe?
* What is the dominant narrative?
* What assumptions are widely accepted?

---

## Hidden Assumptions

Identify:

* Assumptions that may be incorrect
* Assumptions that lack evidence
* Assumptions that depend on perfect execution

Questions:

* What must be true for the consensus to remain valid?
* What could invalidate the consensus?

---

## Blind Spot Search

Look for:

* Ignored risks
* Ignored opportunities
* Emerging trends
* Structural changes
* Regulatory shifts
* Competitive threats

---

## Contrarian Analysis

Questions:

* If the majority is wrong, why?
* What scenario would surprise most investors?
* What outcome has the lowest market attention?

---

## Second-Order Effects

Evaluate:

* Immediate consequences
* Secondary consequences
* Tertiary consequences

Avoid stopping at first-order thinking.

---

## Opportunity Discovery

Identify:

* Non-obvious upside drivers
* Mispriced probabilities
* Underappreciated catalysts

---

## Final Assessment

Determine:

* Largest Blind Spot
* Most Overlooked Opportunity
* Most Overlooked Risk
* Most Mispriced Assumption
