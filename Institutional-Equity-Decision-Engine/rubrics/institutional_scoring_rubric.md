# Institutional Scoring Rubric

## Objective

Convert qualitative analysis into a structured institutional score.

The score is a decision-support tool.

It is not a prediction tool.

---

## Scoring Scale

Each category receives a score from:

* 1 = Very Weak
* 2 = Weak
* 3 = Neutral
* 4 = Strong
* 5 = Exceptional

---

## Category 1 — Fundamentals

Evaluate:

* Revenue Growth
* Earnings Growth
* Margin Quality
* Guidance Quality
* Business Strength

Score:

1–5

Weight:

25%

---

## Category 2 — Valuation

Evaluate:

* Relative Valuation
* Growth vs Valuation
* Expectations Risk
* Margin of Safety

Score:

1–5

Weight:

15%

---

## Category 3 — Technical Structure

Evaluate:

* Trend
* Momentum
* Volume
* Market Structure

Score:

1–5

Weight:

15%

---

## Category 4 — Institutional Positioning

Evaluate:

* Ownership Quality
* Positioning
* Crowding Risk
* Liquidity

Score:

1–5

Weight:

15%

---

## Category 5 — Risk Profile

Evaluate:

* Fundamental Risk
* Valuation Risk
* Macro Risk
* Tail Risk

Score:

1–5

Weight:

15%

---

## Category 6 — Opportunity Asymmetry

Evaluate:

* Upside Potential
* Downside Risk
* Risk-Reward Profile
* Mispricing Potential

Score:

1–5

Weight:

15%

---

## Weighted Score

Calculate:

(Fundamentals × 25%)
+
(Valuation × 15%)
+
(Technical Structure × 15%)
+
(Institutional Positioning × 15%)
+
(Risk Profile × 15%)
+
(Opportunity Asymmetry × 15%)

---

## Interpretation

### 4.50 – 5.00

Elite Opportunity

Very High Conviction

---

### 4.00 – 4.49

High Conviction

Strong Candidate

---

### 3.50 – 3.99

Attractive

Worth Monitoring

---

### 3.00 – 3.49

Neutral

Mixed Signals

---

### 2.00 – 2.99

Weak

Significant Concerns

---

### Below 2.00

Avoid

Poor Risk-Reward

---

## Final Requirement

The final recommendation must reference:

* Total Score
* Highest Scoring Category
* Lowest Scoring Category
* Key Risk
* Key Opportunity

before reaching a conclusion.
